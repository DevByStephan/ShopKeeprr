The back office

Run the whole shop.
Not just the checkout.

Orders, customers, catalog, reports and payments live together. The details are handled, so you can spend your time on the part customers see.

Orders & fulfilment

One queue for paid, pending and fulfilled orders, with status history and clean refund handling.

Catalog & inventory

Variants, tier prices, stock movements, categories, coupons and the product pages that connect them.

Payments & refunds

Payments with verified webhooks, idempotent events and refunds that update the order.

Reports & exports

Revenue, tax, shipping and product performance, with CSV exports when you need to take the numbers further.

Customers & subscriptions

Accounts, trade applications, subscription state and the customer history that makes support easier.

Update tooling

Optional signed updates are included for hosts that publish a manifest, with database and file backups and rollback.
